Salad

Description

A fresh and healthy salad with crisp greens, tomatoes, cucumbers, and a tangy dressing.

Ingredients

  • Lettuce
  • Tomatoes
  • Cucumbers
  • Olive oil
  • Lemon juice
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Chop all vegetables.
  2. Mix them in a large bowl.
  3. Drizzle with olive oil and lemon juice.
  4. Season with salt and pepper.
