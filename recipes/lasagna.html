Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish with layers of pasta, meat sauce, and cheese baked to perfection.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Garlic
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Cook the lasagna noodles according to package instructions.
  2. Brown the ground beef and mix it with tomato sauce.
  3. Layer the noodles, meat sauce, and cheese.
  4. Bake in a preheated oven at 375°F for 45 minutes.
  5. Let cool for 10 minutes before serving.
